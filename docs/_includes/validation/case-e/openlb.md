A point is scored only where every point at its height in an empty-domain control is within 5 % of the imposed log law. In an empty domain 256 m wide, 320 m tall and 1100 m long with a log law fitted to Case E's approach-flow profile below 60 m, 350–650 m from the inlet (where the district's points sit), $$U$$ at 2 m reads 1.03 (1.012–1.046) of the log law with OpenLB's ground wall model and the rough law, averaged over half the time the 2 m layer takes to cross the domain, and 0.23 (0.11–0.34) with the bounce-back ground. Averaged over two crossings, the wall model reads 1.02 (0.985–1.0504), five points just over 5 %; over the second crossing alone, 1.01 (0.94–1.06). In a domain 32 m wide and 80 m tall the same wall model read 0.94 (0.87–1.07). The runs of Cases A and B predate the wall model. These controls ran on `openlb-wind:1.9.3-r4` and `-r5`, before `1.9.3-r6` moved the lattice's ground row half a cell down.
