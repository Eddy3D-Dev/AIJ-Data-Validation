A point is scored only where every point at its height in an empty-domain control is within 5 % of the imposed log law. In an empty domain 96 m wide with a log law fitted to Case E's approach-flow profile below 60 m, at that lattice, 350–650 m from the inlet (where the district's points sit), $$U$$ at 2 m reads 0.99 (0.98–0.99) of the log law with FluidX3D's ground wall function and a steady inflow, every point within 5 %, and 0.36 (0.20–0.56) with the bounce-back ground. In the 32 m wide domain of the OpenLB control the wall function reads 0.98 (0.92–1.01): beyond about 450 m from the inlet the narrow domain varies across its span. With synthetic inlet turbulence the wide domain reads 1.01 (0.96–1.03). The Niigata district fits on the GPU of the A and B runs at up to about 1.6 cells per 2 m (an estimate from the lattice sizes). The runs of Cases A and B predate the wall function.
