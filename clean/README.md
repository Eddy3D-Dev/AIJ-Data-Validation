# clean/ — the AIJ UWE benchmark (Cases A–E) as one queryable table

Wind-tunnel reference data of the Architectural Institute of Japan *Urban Wind Environment*
benchmark, Cases A–E (Kikumoto et al. 2026, JAR 9(1) e70083), re-released in **one schema**: SI
units, metres, one coordinate frame per case, NaN for not-measured, every configuration and wind
direction as rows. Licence **CC BY 4.0** (the data belongs to its collectors — cite them, section 6).
Every table, geometry file, the manifest, `checks.json` and `build.log` are regenerated by `build.py`
from the published files and never edited by hand; the documents (this README, `SCHEMA.md`,
`QUESTIONS.md`) and `build.py` itself are written by hand.

If you are an agent: read this file, then `manifest.json`, then the table. Read the *frame* and the
*known data issues* of a case before its numbers — they differ per case, and three of them invalidate a
comparison if they are not accounted for (section 4).

## 2. One-liners

```python
import json, pandas as pd
pts = pd.read_parquet("clean/points.parquet")          # or pd.read_csv("clean/points.csv")
m   = json.load(open("clean/manifest.json", encoding="utf-8"))
units = json.load(open("clean/units.json", encoding="utf-8"))["points"]

# Case A/B velocity field on the vertical centre plane
pts.query("case_id == 'A' and y == 0")[["x", "z", "U", "V", "W", "k"]]

# Case C: pedestrian scalar speed, tall centre block, wind along +x, as a 13 x 13 map
c = pts.query("case_id == 'C' and config == '2D' and wind_direction_deg == 0")
c.pivot(index="y", columns="x", values="Vs")

# Case D: the 33 points where both instruments overlap (thermistor Vs vs split-fibre |U| and k)
d = pts.query("case_id == 'D' and wind_direction_deg == 0")
T = d[d.instrument == "thermistor"][["point_id", "Vs"]].rename(columns={"Vs": "Vs_T"})
both = T.merge(d[d.instrument == "split_fibre"], on="point_id")        # 33 shared points
mag = (both.U**2 + both.V**2 + both.W**2) ** 0.5
(both.Vs_T / mag).mean(), both.Vs_T.mean() / mag.mean()                # 1.50 (mean of ratios), 1.34 (ratio of means)

# Case E: before/after construction, every direction, as columns
e = pts.query("case_id == 'E'").pivot_table(index=["point_id", "wind_direction"], columns="config", values="velocity_ratio")
(e.ac - e.bc).groupby(level="wind_direction").apply(lambda s: s.abs().mean()).sort_values()

# The wind vector for a (case, direction) — never derive it from the angle yourself
dirs = pd.read_csv("clean/directions.csv")
dirs.query("case_id == 'C' and wind_direction_deg == 45")[["flow_dir_x", "flow_dir_y", "status"]]

# Frame, units, inflow and provenance of one case
m["cases"]["D"]["frame"]; m["cases"]["D"]["inflow"]["log_law"]; m["cases"]["D"]["provenance"]
```

Parquet files carry the units in their metadata too:
`pyarrow.parquet.read_schema("clean/points.parquet").metadata[b"units"]`.

Regenerate: `python clean/build.py` (in the environment of `requirements.lock` at the repository root, or
with everything else by `scripts/build_all.sh`; ~20 s). It exits non-zero if any check fails. `python clean/build.py --check`
re-runs the checks and confirms the on-disk tables are what the sources produce (cell for cell, CSV
and Parquet). The output is byte-deterministic (two builds: 49 files identical), so `git diff` after
a rebuild shows exactly what changed and why. The checks are known to be able to fail: with B's
mm→m and V flip removed and C's y axis mirrored in a scratch copy, the extents, lateral-sign,
legacy-workbook and WD = 45° symmetry checks all go red (negative control run 2026-10-14).

## 4. Known data issues — what the published files do that this release corrects, and what it cannot

**A**
- Turbulence is published as **rms**; the table holds **variances** (rms²) so A and B share one column meaning. k = ½ Σ variances to 2e-3 (publication rounding).
- Points are on the y ≤ 0 half only (symmetry convention). V is positive toward +y.

**B**
- Coordinates are **millimetres** in `RS_caseB.csv` (`x[mm]`) while velocities are m/s. Converted to metres here. Read as metres, every probe lands 1000× too far out.
- **The published V is positive toward −y** (V = +3.630 just outside the y = −100 mm flank where the flow must deflect outward; Case A reads −2.893 there). Flipped here so V is positive toward +y like A and D. Scored without the flip, B's V anti-correlates with a model at R ≈ −0.9.
- **Seven locations were measured twice**: the foot of each vertical-section profile (points 12, 43, 56, 69, 82, 95, 108 at z = 12.5 mm, y = 0) and the horizontal plane's centreline (110, 147, 160, 173, 186, 199, 212). Both rows are kept under their own point numbers; |ΔU| is 0.05–0.14 m/s (mean 0.105) — a free repeatability estimate, and a trap for anyone keying on coordinates.

**C**
- `CB` is **text** (`0D`/`1D`/`2D`) and the header ends with a trailing comma; here `config`.
- 1,080 rows = **9 configurations** (3 centre blocks × 3 directions) sharing one 120-point set. The 25 centre nodes are absent even for 0D (grid held constant); 24 points lie exactly on a building face plane (|x| or |y| = 0.30) in the gap between blocks — fluid points, but a probe's cell lookup is delicate there.
- Point numbers run 4…132 with gaps 34–37, 46, 55, 100–102 (and 1–3 below the range), exactly the readme's list.
- **Rotation sense:** in the CSV frame WD turns the wind from +x toward **−y** (measured: the 45° field mirrors about y = −x at 0.10–0.13 m/s, not about y = +x at 0.5–0.8). Figure 3 of the data paper draws it toward +y. Invisible at WD = 0; decides everything at 22.5° and 45°. `QUESTIONS.md` Q1.
- Vs is an **omnidirectional scalar** mean speed. No value of 1,080 is below 0.13 U_H; a RANS |U| in a sheltered street goes far lower. Compare the bracket |U| ≤ Vs ≤ √(|U|² + 2k), not one number; Case D measures the gap (1.34 ratio of means).
- The legacy workbook's velocity ratios are not exactly Vs / U(0.02): Vs / ratio spans 2.41–2.45 m/s. The 2-dp Vs of the CSV is the released value; do not "recover" a third decimal from the ratios.
- Repeatability from the mirror pairs: mean |ΔVs| 0.09–0.13 m/s = 0.024–0.036 U_H.

**D**
- The repository clone had only the workbook; the official `RS-T_caseD.csv`, `RS-S_caseD.csv`, `AF_caseD.csv`, readme and `MP_caseD.png` are on Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15402668 and are now in `sources/zenodo_caseD/`. The CSVs are in metres; the workbook in mm; they agree to 4e-16.
- Unmeasured = `-999.9` in the CSVs, `-` in the workbook: NaN here. 12 thermistor points in the band around the tower are unmeasured in every direction (66 of 78 measured); 45 of the 78 points were never measured by the split fibre and are simply absent from its rows.
- `RS-S_caseD.csv`'s z header is the **full-width** character `ｚ[m]` (U+FF5A) — a reader keyed on `z` misses it.
- The split-fibre `Vs` column (and the workbook's "Scalar Vel.") is √(U²+V²+W²) to 1e-15 — the vector magnitude, not a thermistor reading — and is dropped. The thermistor's readings at those 33 points are in the thermistor rows; they average 1.34× the magnitude, and √(|U|² + 2k) from the split-fibre data reproduces them to FB +0.06.
- The 0° thermistor field is an **exact** y-mirror (33 pairs, ΔVs = 0): half was measured and mirrored, so only 33 of 66 values are independent. The array is exactly y-symmetric, so the **22.5°/45° rotation sense is undecidable from the data**; the sense in `directions.csv` follows Figure 4 of the data paper and is corroborated by solved Eddy3D fields at both headings (`QUESTIONS.md` Q2).
- The published `BD_caseD.stl` has 576 duplicate facets and all 83 roofs wound inward (signed volume half the true one); `geometry.stl` here is the 83 boxes, watertight, analytic volume.
- The measurement plane z/H = 0.02 sits below the 0.02…2H window a log law is usually fitted over; the two fits in `manifest.json` differ by 5× in z₀ and by 20 points in U at the plane. A log-law inlet cannot be right at both the plane and aloft; the inlet k implied by atmBoundaryLayer (0.39–0.65 m²/s²) is below the tunnel's 0.53–0.75.
- The repository's `raw/cases/case-d/case-d-eddy3d-prior-comparison.xlsx` is a prior attempt at R = +0.10 against the thermistor — a frame or geometry error, not a result.

**E (provisional geometry — see manifest `status_note`)**
- Coordinates are **full-scale metres**, +x east, +y north, origin at the centre of the area; wind directions are compass FROM-directions (N blows toward −y). z = 2 m (0.008 m model).
- The ratio is normalised by **U(15.9 m) = 3.935 m/s** (the Niigata met-station height), not by U_H and not by UR = 7.8 m/s. Divide by UR and every prediction is low by 1.98×; the repository's prior `raw/cases/case-e/case-e-eddy3d-prior-comparison.xlsx` did exactly that.
- **Quantised:** every ratio is a multiple of 0.0198204 (55 levels); resolution ±0.0099 = 3 % of the median, 10 % of the smallest values. Any bar tighter than one quantum measures the publisher's rounding. Zenodo's `RS_caseE.csv` is the workbook rounded further, to 2 dp; this release uses the workbook.
- The official `BD_caseE.stl` (now in `sources/zenodo_caseE/`) is **model scale** (×250 here), **not watertight** (5,253 open edges), after-construction only. The before-construction geometry here is an Eddy3D reconstruction from the 3dm with no published source.
- **Point 50 at (−45.5, −45.0) is 0.17 m inside a wall of the official STL** and 2.06 m clear in the 3dm; the other 79 are in open space in both (recorded as a warning in `checks.json`; `QUESTIONS.md` Q3). A probe at point 50 against the official STL will fail or read inside a building.
- Do not take sensor coordinates from the 3dm's `probes` layer (hand-placed spheres, median 2.3 m off); the workbook's `Geometry&Points` sheet is authoritative and all 80 of its points pass the open-space test, while a 10° rotation of the cloud puts 26 inside buildings.
- Construction signal: mean (ac − bc) = +0.097; largest for SW (mean |Δ| 0.216), smallest for SSE (0.093).

**All thermistor cases (C, D, E)**: the measured quantity is the mean of the instantaneous *scalar* speed; steady RANS gives the magnitude of the *mean* vector, which is lower by construction in a turbulent canopy. Report the bracket, and say which end you are quoting.

## Cases J, K, L — Tokyo Polytechnic University non-isothermal and cube-array experiments

Three Zenodo records of Yoshie and co-workers (J 10.5281/zenodo.15447887, K 10.5281/zenodo.15448050, L
10.5281/zenodo.15487637), cleaned the same way as A–E: one table (`cases/J|K|L/points.csv`), one wind direction (0°, flow toward
+x), SI units, the approach-flow profile, the geometry, `directions.csv`, and the checks below in `checks.json`. Frames,
reference values and extra files are in `SCHEMA.md` ("Cases J, K, L"); what the sources cannot settle is in `QUESTIONS.md`
(QJ1 – QJ5, QK1 – QK6, QL1 – QL6).

| case | what | rows | quantities measured | source |

| K | 9 × 9 cubes H = 0.06 m, pitch 0.12 m, isothermal; U_H = 2.33 m/s | 203 in the streets and over the first rows | U V W uu vv ww k, C c_rms | 5 mm floor outlet at x = −0.15, 100 % ethylene |

**Traps found, with the numbers**

*J*

- **Temperature is kelvin** in `RS_caseJ.csv`, `AF_caseJ.csv` and `BD-ST_caseJ.csv`; the table carries °C (the workbook's θ_f = 45.8 °C is the CSV's 318.95 K).
- **The CSV is quantised.** It equals the workbook's non-dimensional values rounded to 10⁻³ (U, V, W) or 10⁻⁴ and multiplied back by U_H = 1.4581 m/s, Δθ = 33.6 K or c0: reproduced with **zero** differing cells in 14 columns × 444 rows (and not by one decimal coarser). So the published heat fluxes sit on a grid of 0.0049 K·m/s (≈ 10 % of a typical value), k on 0.00021 m²/s². The table uses the workbook.
- **c0 = 12.283 ppm contains the 5 % ethylene fraction** (workbook cell; = 0.05 q / (U_H H²) to 1e-15). Dividing by q / (U_H H²) alone gives 245.7 ppm, a factor of 20. The data paper states a c0 for Cases H (45.2 ppm, pure) and I (39.2 ppm, 10 % ethane, fraction included) but not for J.
- **One location twice:** No. 1 and No. 153 are the same row (−0.16, 0, 0.004). The CSV merged the other 21 locations the workbook lists in two sheets; both rows are kept (as for B's repeats), but a score over the table counts that point twice.
- **σ and variance columns disagree:** the workbook's `<u′²>` columns are not always the square of its σ columns (up to 3.2 % in uu, 47 of 443 locations with a component off by more than 1 %). `k` is built from the variances (relative error 9e-5), which the table uses.
- **Expanded uncertainty is in the workbook** next to every quantity (non-dimensional units; none in the CSV): median 0.031 m/s for U, 0.27 K for T, 0.010 K·m/s for u′θ′, about 1 ppm (0.083 c0) plus ≈ 5 % of the value for C. The mean concentration does not exceed its own uncertainty at 197 of the 465 workbook rows, and four CSV rows read exactly 0.0 (analyser baseline). **193 uncertainty cells of the w′θ′ flux in the sheet `xy-plane2` are an Excel fill series** (1.0037, 2.0037 … 193.0037 = 0.0037 + a running integer), not values. Summary and locations: `manifest.json → cases.J.extra_measurements.expanded_uncertainty`.
- **The empty-tunnel profile changes along the tunnel:** U at z = 0.019 H is 0.33 U_H at x/H = −2.5 (the published `AF_caseJ.csv`), 0.58 at x/H = 0 and 0.50 at +2.5; at z = H it is 1.00, 1.03, 1.04. The workbook has all three (`cases/J/approach_flow_full.csv`).
- The readme's Reynolds number 43,000 is Case H's (3.23 m/s × 0.2 m); U_H H / ν = 1.6·10⁴ as the data paper says.
- The readme's `T_b = 285.32 K` is the approach-flow air temperature at z = H (workbook θ_H = 12.17 °C), not the building, whose measured surface (121 points, `BD-ST_caseJ.csv`) averages 24.47 °C (22.0 – 28.9 °C).
- No shear stresses at the points (only the approach flow has uv, uw); the lateral sign of V is confirmed (V < 0 on the windward y < 0 flank, > 0 behind).

*K*

- **The workbook normalises with another building:** H = 0.2 m, UH = 4.94009 m/s, C0 = 18.218 ppm (its normalised columns reproduce its dimensional ones exactly with those), not the cube's H = 0.06 m, U_H = 2.33 m/s. With the readme's numbers c0 = 429.18 ppm, 23.6 times larger; the table uses that and the manifest gives both (`QUESTIONS.md` QK1). The approach-flow file gives 2.24 m/s at z = 0.06 m, 4 % under the stated 2.33.
- **`AF_caseK.csv` is Case H's file** (17 rows, identical); the data paper says the approach flows were the same.
- **No point numbers**: `RS_caseK.csv` has none; `point_id` is the file's row order. The header has a space before every unit (`x (m)`).
- The CSV is the workbook rounded (velocities 3 dp, C 2 dp) with **k recomputed from the rounded rms**; the workbook k is used (max difference 1.2·10⁻³).
- The workbook's units: x, y in mm in both data sheets, z in mm in `Measurement data` but **metres** in `Inflow` (headed `z(mm)`).
- The workbook's `<usc>` ("velocity magnitude", never below |U|: 1.03 – 3.9 × |U|) is not carried.
- The array: 81 cubes on a 0.12 m pitch is read from the drawings; the points bound the pitch to 0.11 – 0.14 m (4 and 8 points fall inside cubes at 0.11 and 0.14).
- **Mirror pairs** (74): U agrees to 2.8 %, k to 5.3 %, but C differs by 44 % on average: the mean concentration is not symmetric. V is a cross-flow offset (even part 0.057 m/s, odd part 0.017 m/s; the empty tunnel has 0.041): its sign cannot be confirmed.
- The data paper's sentence "approximately 30 points at eight heights downwind of the line source" does not describe K (203 points, five heights, point source).

*L*

- **`RS_caseL.csv`'s `T(K)` column is degrees Celsius** (12.85 – 29.86 = the workbook's θ to 0.005); `AF_caseL.csv`'s is kelvin (283 – 295). Read as kelvin the points would be 260 K colder than the floor.
- The CSV is the workbook rounded to the printed decimals, checked for all 20 columns; `Usc` of the workbook is |U| (derived) and `tc` (⟨θ′c′⟩, K·ppm) has no schema column.
- **Geometry from `BD_caseL.stl`:** 126 cubes of 0.06 m, 14 rows along x (x = −0.96 … 0.66) by 9 columns across y (±0.51), pitch 0.12 m; watertight, outward, no duplicates. The data paper's "9 rows by 14 columns" counts the other way round. The STL's source patch is 0.04 m × 1.02 m; the readme and data paper say 0.037 m × 1.2 m.
- **The readme's file list names `AF_caseK.csv` and `RS_caseK.csv`; its "building surface temperature T_b = 14.8 °C" is the air temperature at H** (workbook θ_H = 14.75 °C; the workbook gives θ_build = 42.2 °C).
- **Layout matches the data paper to the point:** 27 locations × 8 heights + 14 × 4 + 24 × 3 = 344.
- **q, ppm and the line width are consistent:** the tracer flux ∫(U C + u′c′) dz through four cross-sections, averaged over the street lines (y = ±0.06) and the cube-column line (y = 0), is 0.98, 1.00, 0.96, 0.86 of q / 1.2 m (the street lines alone carry 1.3 – 1.9, the column line 0.2 – 0.4).
- **Not mirror-symmetric:** the same point at +y and −y differs in U by 26 %, in k by 23 %, in C by 36 % on average, and at z = 0.15 m (0.09 m above the roofs) U is 0.11 m/s lower and T 1.3 K higher at +y: a lateral gradient of the tunnel flow. Do not assume symmetry in a model.
- The workbook, unlike J's, carries no expanded uncertainty; the approach-flow CSV lists the profile from the top and has no k.

**Checks added** (names in `checks.json`; the cases' modules are `case_j.py`, `case_k.py`, `case_l.py`, shared code in `jkl_common.py`):

- *Counts and layout:* row counts and point numbers; J's 465 workbook rows = 443 locations (22 shared, identical) + the one duplicate; L's census against the data paper (27 × 8 + 14 × 4 + 24 × 3); K's and L's y and z levels against the MP drawings; coordinates of CSV and workbook equal.
- *CSV against workbook:* J reproduces the CSV exactly as round(round(ratio, 3|4) × scale, d) (negative control: one decimal coarser fails); K and L within half a unit of the last printed digit in every column; every normalised workbook column reproduces its dimensional one (K: with H = 0.2 m, UH = 4.94 m/s; L: with the cell constants); K's CSV k equals the half-sum of the rounded rms.
- *Physics:* k = ½ Σ variances (all three); the signs of the turbulent fluxes are those of an unstable layer (w′θ′ > 0, T falling with height, u′w′ < 0; J, L); c0 recomputed from q, gas fraction, U_H and H against the workbook cell (J, L) and K's two definitions; Rb recomputed from the workbook's temperatures (J: −0.0857 against −0.0856; L: −0.0344 against −0.035); temperatures against the readme and data paper; U_H from the profile (J 1.46, L 1.3394, K 2.24 against the stated 2.33: a warning); tracer flux through cross-sections against q (L: 0.86 – 1.0 of q / W; K: a lower bound, 0.40 – 0.67 of q); concentration peaks at the source (J, K: the point 4 mm above the outlet; L: on the line).
- *Geometry and frame:* no sensor inside a building and a shifted-points control that does land inside (J, K, L); K's pitch scan; L's STL = the 14 × 9 grid, watertight, outward, volume 126 H³; J's surface-temperature points on the building faces and their mean equal the workbook's θ_build; V sign (J); WD = 0 flow along +x (all).
- *Warnings* (documented, never fail the build): the variance/σ discrepancy, the fill-series uncertainty cells, the mean concentration below its uncertainty and the profile change along the tunnel (J); the stated U_H, C not mirror-symmetric and V an offset (K); readme building temperature, source patch size and mirror asymmetry (L); the readme's Reynolds number (J).
